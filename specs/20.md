## Problem

The local compose profile (spec 3, PR #19) works with its defaults. Its conclude review found a P2: dex's static config fixes the redirect URI and issuer. Overriding `OMNIGENT_PORT`, `OMNIGENT_OIDC_CLIENT_ID` or `DEX_PORT` in `.env` therefore breaks the OIDC login, with a redirect mismatch or an issuer mismatch. The overrides should flow into dex's config, through a rendered config or environment expansion. `scripts/smoke-local.sh` should prove one non-default port set.

Member of holetz/forge epic 1 (follow-up of S13).
